# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them.
- Give useful feedback about the work, not attacks on the person.
- Protect other people's private information.

Harassment, threats, trolling, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, and publishing another
person's private information are not accepted.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project
spaces. The maintainer may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently ban a
participant who violates it.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile:
https://github.com/gititya. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept private as far as
practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
